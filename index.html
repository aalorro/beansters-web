How to play

  • Every row reads left to right and every column reads top to bottom. Each must equal the target at its end.
  • No operator precedence. 2 + 3 × 4 is worked as (2 + 3) × 4 = 20, one step at a time.
  • Every step must stay a whole number, so a division only works if it comes out exact.
  • Pick a tile from the tray, then tap an empty slot (or tap the slot first). Tap a placed tile to lift it back out. You can also drag beans directly onto the board.
  • The timer starts on your first move. Take your time reading the board — the clock only begins when you place your first bean.
  • You have 3 lives ♥♥♥. Every time a fully filled row or column evaluates to the wrong total, you lose one life. Lose all three and you'll sit out a 5-minute cool-off. Lives reset at the start of every game.
  • Later levels add decoy tiles that fit nowhere, blank operators you choose yourself, negative running totals, and larger grids.
  • Any arrangement that satisfies every target counts. Three stars: no hints, under par time.
  • Levels never run out. Your progress is saved in this browser.

About

  • BEANSTERS is a colorful bean-themed math puzzle. Place numbered beans into the grid so every row and column equals its target. Math is evaluated strictly left to right — no operator precedence.
  • Levels scale in difficulty: larger grids, decoy tiles, hidden operators, and negative numbers as you progress. Levels never run out.
  • Privacy. All your progress (username, level, score, stars) is stored only in your browser's local storage under the key numeral-loom-v1. Nothing is ever sent to any server. Clearing your browser's site data will reset your progress.
  • Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no tracking, no cookies.
